The plugin's default archive is `~/.tenet/recordings` on the Pi thread's BB machine; optional per-machine absolute paths go in its `recordingDirectories` setting.

During the real trial Pi temporarily loaded this task worktree, then its global package entry was restored to `../../workspace/Tenet`.
