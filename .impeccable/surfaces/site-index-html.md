# Marketing homepage

Primary target: `site/index.html`. Mode: Persuade. Audience: developers deciding whether to put TENET in a Pi session. They should understand that owner-written policy is assessed against tool calls, observe mode reports without stopping a call, and enforcement requires opt-in. Primary action: read the repository documentation. Proof: one explicitly illustrative policy, shell call, and observed finding. No fabricated customer, benchmark, or safety guarantee.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The policy and the next tool call meet in plain sight. Refuse the generic feature-card landing page and the discarded metaphor-heavy concepts.

OWN-WORLD: Pure white, near-black humanist sans, muted gray secondary text, one fine rule, black pill action. No logo symbol, accent block, illustration, gradient, or fake app frame. This marketing identity is separate from the existing inspector design.

STORY: The opening states the promise, demonstrates one assessment, and sends visitors to the real docs. A short continuation explains observe versus opt-in enforce and the limits of semantic evaluation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At desktop width, small wordmark and two links sit at top. Large quiet space precedes a two-line 56px headline on the left. Supporting sentence and docs action follow. A compact unframed assessment with top hairline sits on the right. On mobile the assessment stacks beneath the action; all content remains readable.

FORM: User-pinned Editorial space, chosen after concept seed 3405a2ad, reroll 4. The selected compositional comp is `.impeccable/mocks/tenet-marketing-editorial-selected.png`. Its observe wording corrects an earlier image's factual mistake. Keep the spatial arrangement, not image pixels.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
